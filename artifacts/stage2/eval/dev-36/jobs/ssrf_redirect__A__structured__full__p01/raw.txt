SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to a redirect
SINK: opening an HTTP connection and reading the response body
GUARD: resolve the hostname to its IP address and raise ValueError if resolution fails; check the scheme and raise ValueError when it is not http or https; follow only the initial request and raise ValueError for every redirect, regardless of the redirect's Location
ORDER: resolve and check the scheme before making the request; capture the initial destination and never follow a redirect
EFFECT: return the response body for status 200
END_PLAN
