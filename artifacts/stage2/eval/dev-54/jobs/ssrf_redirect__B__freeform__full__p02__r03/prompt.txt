Plan:

Normalize the requested URL and raise ValueError if it escapes the current effective directory, then follow at most five Location headers, resolving each Location against the current URL and raising ValueError unless the resolved Location remains inside the current effective directory. Accept HTTP on port 80 and HTTPS on port 443 only, raising ValueError for every other scheme or port.
