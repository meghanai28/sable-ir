SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to a redirect
SINK: opening an HTTP connection and reading the response body
GUARD: resolve the hostname to its IP address and raise ValueError if resolve_host returns no IP; check the request scheme and raise ValueError unless it is http or https; follow only the initial destination and raise ValueError for every redirect response, regardless of whether the redirected URL appears public
ORDER: resolve_host and scheme check before opening the connection; do not follow a redirect
EFFECT: return the response body for status 200
END_PLAN
