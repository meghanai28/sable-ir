SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting an archive onto the filesystem

ORDER: check components and containment before extracting; return False if any check fails
EFFECT: extract the archive's regular files and directories only, never following symbolic or hard links
