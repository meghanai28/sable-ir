The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments. The dangerous operation is extracting a tar archive. Reject symbolic links and hard links, even those whose targets appear to stay inside the destination; a symbolic link whose target remains inside the destination must still be rejected. Validate components of dest_path before opening or extracting the archive, and let a failed validation or extraction complete failure return False without writing any archive member outside dest_path. Return True only when extraction completes successfully.
