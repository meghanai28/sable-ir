SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject absolute dest_path; normalize dest_path and raise an error if it escapes the root; walk each member of the archive, resolve its target, and raise an error unless the fully resolved target remains inside dest_path; a symbolic link or hard link whose resolved target stays inside dest_path may be extracted
ORDER: validate components and link targets before opening or writing any archive content; capture and log the resolved target for each member
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when all members are extracted safely
