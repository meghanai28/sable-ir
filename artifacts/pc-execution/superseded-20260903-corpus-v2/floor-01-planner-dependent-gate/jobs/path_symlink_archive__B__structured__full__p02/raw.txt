SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject absolute dest_path; normalize dest_path and raise an error if it escapes the root; walk each member of the archive, resolve its target, and accept it only when the fully resolved target remains inside dest_path; a symbolic link or hard link whose resolved target stays inside the destination may be created; validate every member and each link before extracting any archive content
ORDER: validate components and links before opening or extracting the archive; capture extraction failures and return False without writing any member
EFFECT: extract the tar archive beneath dest_path and return True only when extraction completes successfully
