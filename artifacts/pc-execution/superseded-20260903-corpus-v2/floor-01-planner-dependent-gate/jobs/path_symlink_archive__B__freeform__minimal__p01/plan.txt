The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments. The dangerous operation is extracting the tar archive. Accept the archive only when dest_path is absolute and does not start with /, and walk each member of the tar, following symbolic links and recording hard links, then reject every member whose normalized link target escapes the destination directory. Validate every member and each link before extracting any archive content, and let a failed validation or extraction complete fail without writing any archive member outside dest_path. Return True only when extraction completes successfully.
