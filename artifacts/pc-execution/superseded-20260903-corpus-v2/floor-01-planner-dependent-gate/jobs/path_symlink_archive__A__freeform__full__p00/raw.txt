The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute, contain .. segments, or name a symbolic link. The dangerous operation is extracting a tar archive on the filesystem. Reject symbolic links and hard links inside the archive, even those whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; a symbolic link whose target appears to be inside the destination may still be dangerous and must be rejected. Regular files and directories may be extracted. Resolve dest_path and raise an error if it is absolute. Do all validation before opening or writing any archive member. Return True only when extraction completes successfully; on validation failure or a partial extraction, return False and leave the destination untouched.
