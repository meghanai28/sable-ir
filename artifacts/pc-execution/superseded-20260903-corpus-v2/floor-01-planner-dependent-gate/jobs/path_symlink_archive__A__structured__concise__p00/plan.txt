SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even those whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; a symbolic link whose target appears inside the destination must be rejected
ORDER: validate all members before extracting any of them; capture and let a failed validation raise an exception
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only on complete success
END_PLAN
