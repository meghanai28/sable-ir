SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member inside the archive, even one whose declared target remains inside dest_path; a symbolic-link whose target points outside the destination must also be rejected. Regular files and directories may be extracted. Do all validation before creating any file or directory. Return False if no members are extracted, and raise an exception if extraction fails.
ORDER: validate members before opening or writing the archive
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when extraction completes successfully
