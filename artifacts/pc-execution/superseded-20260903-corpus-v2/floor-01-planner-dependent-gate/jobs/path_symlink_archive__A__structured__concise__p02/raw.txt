SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even ones whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; a symbolic link whose target points inside the destination must be rejected
regular files and directories may be extracted
do not create the destination directory before validation; let a failed extraction fail
return True only when extraction completes successfully
return False without writing any archive member outside dest_path
ORDER: validate components of the tar before opening it; check each member after it is read
EFFECT: extract the validated archive beneath dest_path and return True
END_PLAN
