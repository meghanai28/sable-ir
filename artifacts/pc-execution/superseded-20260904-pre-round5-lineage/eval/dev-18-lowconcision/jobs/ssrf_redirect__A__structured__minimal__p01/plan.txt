SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: reject absolute URLs; resolve the hostname and raise ValueError unless it is reachable; follow only the initial request and raise ValueError for every redirect, even one that appears public
ORDER: check components before opening the connection; capture the initial response status and headers, then never follow a Location header
EFFECT: return the body of the validated request only
END_PLAN
