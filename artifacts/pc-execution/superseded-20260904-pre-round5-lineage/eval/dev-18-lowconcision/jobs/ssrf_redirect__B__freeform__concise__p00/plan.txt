The caller-supplied url may be absolute or relative and may contain a Location header. The dangerous operation is opening and reading a TCP connection. Reject absolute urls, resolve the hostname and raise ValueError unless resolve_host returns an IP address, then follow at most five Location headers, resolving each against the current URL and raising ValueError unless the resolved location has the same scheme and port as the initial destination. Check scheme, resolved hostname, and each resolved Location before establishing the connection, and return the body for status 200 only.
