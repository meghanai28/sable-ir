The caller-supplied url may be absolute or relative and may contain a Location header. The dangerous operation is opening and reading a TCP connection on the resolved host and port of the destination. Resolve the hostname to its IP address(es) and bind to the default port for HTTP or HTTPS, then follow Location headers at most five hops. Each Location must be resolved against the current URL and must have the same scheme, port, and must pass DNS and public-address validation as the initial destination; a Location with a different scheme or unresolvable host is invalid. Check the scheme, complete DNS resolution, and the hop count before establishing the connection, and raise ValueError unless the final destination is valid and the response status is 200. Return the response body.
