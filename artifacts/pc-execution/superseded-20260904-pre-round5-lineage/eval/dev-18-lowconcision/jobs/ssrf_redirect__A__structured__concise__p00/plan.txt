SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to a redirect
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: reject absolute URLs; resolve the hostname and raise ValueError unless it is reachable; accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port; follow the initial destination only and never follow a redirect, even one that appears public
ORDER: check components before opening the connection; capture the final resolved hostname and raise ValueError if resolution fails
EFFECT: return the body text of the initial request's 200 response
