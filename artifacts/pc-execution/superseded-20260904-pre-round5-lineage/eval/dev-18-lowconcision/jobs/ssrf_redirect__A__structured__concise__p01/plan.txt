SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port, resolve the hostname, and raise ValueError unless the resolved IP addresses are finite; follow the initial destination only and reject every redirect response, even one that appears public
ORDER: resolve and check the destination before opening the connection; capture the final destination after resolving and do not follow redirects
EFFECT: return the body of the validated request whose status is 200
