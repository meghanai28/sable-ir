SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to a redirect
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: reject absolute URLs; resolve the hostname and raise ValueError unless it is reachable; follow only the initial request and never follow a redirect, even one that appears public
ORDER: check components before establishing the connection
EFFECT: return the body of the initial response whose status is 200
END_PLAN
