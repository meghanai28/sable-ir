SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to a redirect
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port and raise ValueError for every other scheme; resolve the requested hostname and raise ValueError unless it resolves; follow the initial public destination but reject every redirect response, even one that appears public
ORDER: check scheme and resolve the hostname before making the network request; capture the initial destination before following redirects and raise ValueError if it is not public
EFFECT: return the body of the validated request whose status is 200
