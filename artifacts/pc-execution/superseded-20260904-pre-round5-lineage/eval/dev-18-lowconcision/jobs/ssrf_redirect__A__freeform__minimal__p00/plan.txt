The caller-supplied url may be absolute, contain query and fragment components, and be resolved through the provided transport. The dangerous operation is opening and reading a TCP connection on the default HTTP port. Reject absolute urls, resolve the hostname and raise ValueError unless it resolves, and follow only the initial request's response status: return the body for status 200 and raise ValueError for every other status, even a redirect. Do not follow a Location header. Check the destination before opening the connection, and let the transport's resolution and connection be the last operation.
