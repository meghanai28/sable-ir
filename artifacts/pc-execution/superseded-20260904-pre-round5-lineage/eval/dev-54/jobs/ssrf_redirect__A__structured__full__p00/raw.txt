SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port; resolve the hostname and raise ValueError if resolution fails; follow the initial destination but never follow a redirect, even one that appears public; check the final destination before reading the body and raise ValueError unless it is on the same scheme and default port as the requested url
ORDER: resolve_host before opening the connection; capture the initial location and reject every redirect response
EFFECT: return the body text of the validated response
