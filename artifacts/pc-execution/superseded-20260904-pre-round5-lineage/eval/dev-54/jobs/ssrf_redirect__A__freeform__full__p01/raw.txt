The caller-supplied url may be absolute or relative and may point to an HTTP or HTTPS resource on its default port. resolve_host(hostname) returns the IP address(es) of the hostname, and transport(url) returns (status, headers, body). The dangerous operation is opening and reading the remote resource. Accept only HTTP or HTTPS URLs on their default ports and raise ValueError for every other scheme. Resolve the hostname to an IP address before making the request, and reject a resolved IP that is loopback or link-local. Check the destination before opening the connection, and return the body only when the status is 200.
