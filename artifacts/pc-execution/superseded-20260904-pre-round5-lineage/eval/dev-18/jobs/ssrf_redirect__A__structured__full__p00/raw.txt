SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response on the network
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port and raise ValueError for every other scheme; resolve the requested hostname and raise ValueError unless it resolves to at least one IP address; follow the initial destination only and reject every redirect response, even one whose Location appears public
ORDER: check scheme and resolve the hostname before opening the connection; capture the final destination after resolution and never follow redirects
EFFECT: return the body text of the validated request's 200 response
