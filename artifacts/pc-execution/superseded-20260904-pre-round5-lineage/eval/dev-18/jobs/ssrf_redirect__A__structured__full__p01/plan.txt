SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening and reading an HTTP response
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port and raise ValueError for every other scheme; resolve the target's hostname and raise ValueError unless resolve_host returns textual IP addresses; follow the Location header only when it is relative and the final resolved destination remains beneath the requested URL's origin; raise ValueError for a redirect response (status 3xx) regardless of its Location value
ORDER: check scheme and resolve the hostname before opening the connection; capture the final resolved destination and check it remains beneath the requested URL's origin; do not follow a redirect
EFFECT: return the body of the response whose status is 200
