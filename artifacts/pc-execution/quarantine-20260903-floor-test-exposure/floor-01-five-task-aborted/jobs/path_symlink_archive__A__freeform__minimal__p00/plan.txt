The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments. The dangerous operation is extracting a tar archive. Reject symbolic links and hard links, even those whose targets remain inside the destination; a symbolic link whose declared target is inside the destination must still be rejected. Do all validation before creating any files on the filesystem, and let a failed extraction leave the destination untouched. Return True only when the extraction completes successfully.
