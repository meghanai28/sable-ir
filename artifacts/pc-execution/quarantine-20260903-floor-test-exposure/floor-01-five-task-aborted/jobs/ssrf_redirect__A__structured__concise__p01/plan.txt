SOURCE: url supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; may be absolute, contain .. segments, or point to any host
SINK: opening an HTTP connection
GUARD: accept only HTTP or HTTPS on the default port; resolve the hostname and raise ValueError if resolution fails; follow redirects only when the initial destination is public and raise ValueError for every redirect response; check the final destination beneath the selected root after symbolic-link resolution and normalization are complete; capture status and body and raise ValueError unless status is 200
ORDER: resolve and check the destination before opening the connection; capture status and body after the connection is established
EFFECT: return the response body text
