SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member inside the archive, even one whose declared target remains inside dest_path; a symbolic-link whose target points outside the destination must also be rejected. Regular files and directories may be extracted. Do all validation before creating any files or directories. Return False if validation fails and never write an archive member outside dest_path.
ORDER: validate the archive before opening or extracting it; capture a failed extraction and let it complete without side effects
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when extraction completes successfully
