SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even those whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; a symbolic-link whose target appears to stay inside the destination must still be rejected; regular files and directories may be extracted
ORDER: validate all members before opening or writing the archive; capture and check each member's declared destination relative to dest_path
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when all members pass validation and extraction completes successfully
