SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may name any location
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even one whose target appears to stay inside dest_path; a symbolic link whose target remains inside the destination must still be rejected; regular files and directories may be extracted
ORDER: check each member before opening or extracting it; do all validation before writing any archive content
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when every member passes validation and extraction completes; return False if validation or extraction fails and never write a member outside dest_path
END_PLAN
