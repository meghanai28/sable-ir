SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: opening and extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject symbolic links and hard links, even those whose targets are inside dest_path; resolve the archive's members before extraction and raise an error if a link member exists
ORDER: validate components of the archive before opening it; capture the original destination and check each extracted member's resolved location
EFFECT: extract the archive's regular files and directories into dest_path only
