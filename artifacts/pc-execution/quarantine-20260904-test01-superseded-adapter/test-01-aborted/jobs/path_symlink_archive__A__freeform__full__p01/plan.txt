The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments. The dangerous operation is extracting a tar archive on the filesystem. Reject absolute dest_path, normalize the destination and raise an error if it escapes the resolved dest_path, and walk each member of the archive, following symbolic links and hard links, and reject every symbolic link or hard link whose target, even if declared inside the archive, would resolve outside the destination. Do all of this validation before opening or extracting any part of the archive, and let a failed validation or extraction complete without writing any archive member outside dest_path. Return True only when the archive has been fully extracted under dest_path.
