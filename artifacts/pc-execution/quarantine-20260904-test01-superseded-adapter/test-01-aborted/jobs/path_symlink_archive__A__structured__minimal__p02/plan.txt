SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, including those whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; a symbolic-link whose target points outside the archive's extraction root must be rejected even if the target does not exist yet
ORDER: check each member before opening or extracting it; do all validation before writing any archive content
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when all members pass validation and are extracted completely
END_PLAN
