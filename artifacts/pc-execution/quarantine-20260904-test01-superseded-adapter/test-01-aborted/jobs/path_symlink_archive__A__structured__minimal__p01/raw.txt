SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may name any location
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, including links whose declared targets remain inside dest_path; regular files and directories may be extracted
ORDER: validate all archive members before opening and extracting the archive; do all validation within the destination directory and never write outside dest_path
EFFECT: return True only when the archive is completely extracted; return False if validation fails or extraction cannot be completed
