SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even one whose target currently points inside dest_path; a symbolic link whose target does not yet exist may still be a link and must be rejected; regular files and directories may be extracted
ORDER: check each member before opening or writing it; capture the archive's contents, resolve the target of each link, and fail the operation unless every link remains inside dest_path
EFFECT: extract the validated archive members beneath dest_path and return True only when all members are successfully extracted
