SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: creating and extracting a tar archive on the filesystem
GUARD: reject absolute dest_path; normalize dest_path and raise if it escapes the root; walk each member of the archive, resolve its normalized target, and raise unless the fully resolved target remains inside dest_path; reject symbolic links and hard links whose resolved target would leave dest_path; validate every member before extracting any content
ORDER: validate components and link targets before extracting the archive
EFFECT: extract the archive beneath dest_path and return True only when all members are fully inside dest_path
