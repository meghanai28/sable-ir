The caller-supplied tar_path and dest_path are untrusted: tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments. The dangerous operation is extracting the tar archive. Reject absolute dest_path, normalize the destination and raise an error if it escapes the resolved dest_path, and reject every symbolic-link and hard-link member, even one whose target remains inside the destination; regular files and directories may be extracted. Check these conditions before opening or writing any archive member, and let a failed validation or extraction complete without writing outside dest_path. Return True only when the archive is fully extracted under dest_path.
