SOURCE: tar_path and dest_path supplied by the caller
TRUST: untrusted; tar_path may name any archive and dest_path may be absolute or contain .. segments
SINK: extracting archive members on the filesystem
GUARD: reject absolute dest_path; normalize dest_path and raise an error if it escapes the root; walk each member of the archive, resolve its link target, and raise an error unless the fully resolved target remains inside dest_path; do all validation before extracting any content; a symbolic link or hard link whose resolved target stays inside dest_path may be extracted
ORDER: check components and links before opening or writing the archive
EFFECT: extract the validated archive members beneath dest_path and return True only when extraction completes without writing outside dest_path
