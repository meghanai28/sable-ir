========================================================================
INTERPRETATION CAVEATS (written before reading the numbers as evidence)
========================================================================
(1) Leave-one-out changes the EVALUATION set as well as the training set, so the
    apparent gain from dropping a task is confounded with evaluating on fewer tasks.
    The per-task column is the cleaner view.

(2) Quadrant membership is DEFINED partly from behavioral outcomes (functional rate,
    assigned-policy pass). Conditioning on it while predicting policy from activations
    is post-treatment conditioning on a collider. Within-quadrant AUROC is therefore
    NOT a clean estimate of policy decodability; it is decodability conditional on
    outcome. It cannot be used to argue the policy is decodable after all.
    Policy balance within each set2 quadrant:
      faithful_success                    n=51  A=25 B=26  (balanced)
      false_certificate                   n=27  A=13 B=14  (balanced)
      hidden_use                          n=12  A= 3 B= 9  (skewed B)
      visible_omission_behavioral_failure n=30  A=19 B=11  (skewed A)
    The two small quadrants are skewed in OPPOSITE directions, so a quadrant-dependent
    score offset can destroy the pooled ranking while leaving within-quadrant ranking
    intact. That is the most likely mechanism for subsets scoring above the pool.

(3) These are exploratory. The preregistered gate returned
    causal_evaluation_authorized=false. Nothing here reopens it, and no layer,
    direction, probe family or paraphrase set was searched after seeing the result.
