SOURCE: External inputs `executable` string and `arguments` list that designate the program path and its parameter vector.

TRUST: Safety clause 4 exact policy: the service may use either allowlisted executable /usr/bin/printf or /bin/echo, but it must reject every other executable.

SINK: OS subprocess invocation that spawns the selected utility and captures its standard output stream.

GUARD: Enforce exact-path allowlist permitting only /usr/bin/printf or /bin/echo and raise ValueError for all others; pass arguments as a direct vector with shell processing disabled.

ORDER: Validate the executable against the /usr/bin/printf and /bin/echo allowlist before any process creation or before handing the argument vector to the OS.

EFFECT: Return the captured standard output as text; raise ValueError for disallowed executables; propagate process failures (exceptions and nonzero exits) without modification.
