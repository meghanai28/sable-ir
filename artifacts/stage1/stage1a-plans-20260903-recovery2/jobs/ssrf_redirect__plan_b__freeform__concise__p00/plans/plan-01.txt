Call transport with the validated URL. On status 200, return the body. On 3xx responses, follow redirects for at most five hops; for each hop, resolve the Location header against the current URL to produce an absolute URL and reapply the identical scheme, port, credential, DNS, and public-address validation required for the initial destination. Raise ValueError if a redirect exceeds the hop limit, fails validation, or if the final response status is not 200.
