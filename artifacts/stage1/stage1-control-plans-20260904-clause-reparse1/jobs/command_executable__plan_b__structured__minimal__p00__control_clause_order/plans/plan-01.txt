SOURCE: `executable` and `arguments` from the caller.
TRUST: Only `/usr/bin/printf` and `/bin/echo` are permitted; every other executable is untrusted and must be rejected.
SINK: Subprocess execution that runs the selected utility and captures its standard output.
GUARD: Raise ValueError if `executable` is not exactly `/usr/bin/printf` or `/bin/echo`; pass `arguments` as a direct vector and disable shell processing.
ORDER: Validate the executable against the allowlist before spawning the subprocess, then execute with the direct argument vector and capture stdout.
EFFECT: Return the captured stdout as text; raise ValueError for a disallowed executable; let process failures propagate.
