Call resolve_host on the hostname and reject the request with ValueError unless every returned IP address is a public, routable address, disallowing loopback, private, link-local, and reserved ranges. Invoke transport with the original URL. If the response status is a redirect, raise ValueError and do not follow the Location header to a subsequent request, regardless of whether that new destination would also appear public. Return the body only when the status is exactly 200; raise ValueError for all other statuses.
